## Goal

Two screens tell a board something untrue today.

- **F-B9.** The "Regulatory exposure: Up to ₹250 crore" figure on the report summary sits directly above a note saying the penalty provisions do not commence until May 2027. The figure needs its commencement date beside it.
- **F-B20.** The comparison page shows "+47.5%" when scope grew from 4 to 93 controls, and "89 changed" counts newly assessed controls as changes. Change must be shown over the controls present in both periods, with added and removed controls counted separately. The live compare page also matches reports by company name; it must match by engagement.

## Page behavior (what the reader sees)

Per framework panel on `/assessments/{id}/compare/{other_id}`:

- Both scores shown with the number of controls each covers ("37% of 4 controls", "84% of 93 controls").
- Change line:
  - Same control set in both periods (added 0, removed 0): the existing score delta, labelled in points ("+3.5 points"), not "%".
  - Scope differs: no overall-score change number. Show "Shared controls: N compared, X improved, Y regressed, Z unchanged, average change +A points" and, beside it, "Newly assessed: M" and "No longer assessed: K", plus one plain sentence: "These reports cover different scopes, so the overall scores are not a like-for-like change."
- "Requirement changes" lists only controls present in both periods whose status changed. Newly assessed controls get their own collapsed or secondary list ("Newly assessed, N") and do not count in "changed".
- The page-level strip counts shared controls only; its wording says so ("Shared controls: ...").
- Header dates: keep what is shown today (the review-period label already comes from the basis); do not change the date logic.
