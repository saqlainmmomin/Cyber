## Goal

Audit findings F-E13 and F-F22 (`tasks/2026-10-13-content-audit.html`, search each id; plan row 2.5 in `docs/plans/2026-10-13-001-single-track-implementation-plan.md`).

- **F-E13.** When scope enforcement turns a requirement into `not_applicable`, only the status changes. The model's gap description, risk level and recommended action are saved beside it and rendered on the conclusions page, the workpaper and the legacy PDF appendix. No reason for the exclusion is stored.
- **F-F22.** A missing risk rating silently becomes "medium" at four places (below). That value feeds the risk matrix, the critical and high counts and the register.

After this WP:
- A not-applicable conclusion carries no gap, no risk level and no recommended action, wherever it comes from (scope enforcement, the model, or a consultant edit).
- A scope-enforced not-applicable conclusion stores its reason in `rationale`, taken from the scope answer that excluded it.
- A missing risk rating is stored blank, shown as `Not rated`, never counted as critical, high or medium, and blocks approval for every outcome except not applicable.

## Decisions (Saqlain, 15 Oct 2026)

| # | Decision | Value |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Where the reason lives | In `Conclusion.rationale`. No new column, no migration. The SoA page (`app/routers/soa.py` about 50 to 54) already reads rationale as the exclusion reason; the parked v2 path does the same. |
| D2 | Reason text | Only for scope-enforced rows (requirement not in `applicable_requirements`). Exactly, keyed by the DPDPA scope answer that is present in `assessment.scope_answers` and equals `"no"`:<br>`SCP.1`: `Out of scope: the organisation does not transfer personal data outside India (scope answer SCP.1: No).`<br>`SCP.2`: `Out of scope: the organisation does not process children's personal data (scope answer SCP.2: No).`<br>`SCP.3`: `Out of scope: the organisation is not designated, or likely to be designated, a Significant Data Fiduciary (scope answer SCP.3: No).`<br>`SCP.5`: `Out of scope: the organisation does not use third-party processors (scope answer SCP.5: No).`<br>Anything else (answer missing, a defaulted answer such as an unanswered SCP.3, a non-DPDPA framework, a requirement in none of the scope sets): `Outside the recorded assessment scope.`<br>"organisation" is the approved spelling here (it matches the scope questions); keep it. |
| D3 | Not-applicable fields | `gaps_identified`, `risk_level` and `recommended_action` are `""` for every `not_applicable` conclusion. For a model-proposed N/A that is not scope-enforced, `rationale` stays the model's text. |
| D4 | Missing risk | Never defaulted. A blank, missing or unrecognized risk from the model is stored as `""` (`Conclusion.risk_level` and `GapItem.risk_level` are NOT NULL; empty string is fine). Normalize case and whitespace first (`" High "` becomes `high`). Display text for blank risk on a non-N/A row: exactly `Not rated`. For N/A rows show nothing in the risk cell (or the existing neutral dash if the layout has one), not `Not rated`. |
| D5 | Approval | A risk level is required for every outcome except `not_applicable`. Keep the existing `INCOMPLETE_CONCLUSION` text unchanged. Edits: when the edited outcome is `not_applicable`, store `""` for gaps, risk and action whatever was submitted; otherwise risk must be in `RISK_LEVELS` (unchanged). The edit schema (`app/schemas/conclusions.py:22`) must accept `""`. |
| D6 | Counting | A blank risk is never counted as critical, high, medium or low. Where a page shows counts by risk for unapproved conclusions (conclusions page, engagement dashboard), add a separate `Not rated` count only if that page already shows per-level counts; do not invent new panels. Approved data cannot hold an unrated gap after D5, so the board risk matrix and severity dashboard keep their four keys; add a test that an unrated gap row is excluded from them. |
| D7 | Old stored rows | No data migration. Readers blank gap, risk and action for `not_applicable` at render time through one shared helper. Old N/A rows keep their stored rationale. |
