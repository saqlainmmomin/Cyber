## Step 0: interview Saqlain before writing any code

**Do not start building.** Read the files under "Read first", then ask Saqlain the questions below in one message, grouped and numbered, each with 2-3 concrete options and your suggested default. Wait for his answers. He will describe the picture he wants the evidence to paint; your job is to turn that into a spec he approves, then build it. If his answers leave a gap, ask again rather than guessing.

Questions to cover (adapt and add your own after reading the code):

1. **The story.** What should an auditor conclude about Veldhara after reading the evidence? Which controls are genuinely strong, which are weak, which look fine on paper but fail when you read the detail (e.g. policy says quarterly access review, the export shows one cycle)? Any deliberate contradictions between documents?
2. **Depth per document.** Today each file is 130-300 characters. Options: (a) realistic short (1-2 pages each), (b) realistic full (policies 5-10 pages with sections, owners, version history, approval blocks), (c) mixed: full for the 3-4 documents the walkthrough leans on, short for the rest. Which documents matter most to him?
3. **Breadth.** Keep the current 8 files, or add more (e.g. supplier register, risk register, training records export, incident log, backup restore log, DPIA, breach notification record, board minutes)? How many in total?
4. **Tabular evidence.** The access-review export today is 40 generated rows. Should it mirror his 220-row synthetic workbook style (record ids, terminated users, admin accounts, exception notes), and which exceptions should be planted? Any other spreadsheets (training completion, asset inventory, vendor list)?
5. **Frameworks.** Current demo covers DPDPA + ISO 27001 (current, prior, interim) and an early NIST CSF assessment. Should the evidence be written to serve all of them, or is one the focus?
6. **Realism markers.** Version tables, approval signatures, document dates (some stale on purpose), owners by role, Indian context (DPDPA, CERT-In 6-hour reporting, RBI if relevant to logistics), screenshots as images, scanned-PDF style for OCR?
7. **Questionnaire and conclusions.** Should the seeded answers, conclusions and findings be rewritten to match the richer evidence, or only the documents change?

Then write the spec at the bottom of this file (section "Agreed spec"), show it to Saqlain, and build only after he says go.
