ADDENDUM for the DPDPA-layer controls (this overrides fw_prompt.txt where they differ).
- Your controls have origin dpdpa. Briefs are in the same briefs folder, regenerated for them.
- Layer rule: these controls are shown only when the privacy law is in scope. You may use the law's plain terms in statements: "data principal", "data fiduciary", "data processor", "consent manager", "significant data fiduciary", "the Board" (the data protection regulator), "personal data breach". Still never write the Act's name, "DPDPA", "DPDP", "Rules", section or rule numbers, or any framework name. No "s.6", no "r.7".
- Numbers and timeframes the law sets (breach intimation periods, erasure inactivity periods, retention minimums, response periods) belong ONLY in key_settings under the `dpdpa:` layer, and the same wording must appear in the text where you mention it. Take them from the signed criteria and curated guidance in the brief. If the brief gives no number, do not invent one. Example key_settings: `dpdpa: report to the Board within 72 hours | dpdpa: keep logs for one year`.
- Remainder slots (ids with number 70 or higher in a non-privacy domain, noted "remainder of UC-xxx"): the statement covers only what the base control does not. It should read naturally next to the base control and must not repeat it.
- question: reuse the curated DPDPA question text in the brief where it is good, trimmed to one obligation (the compound ones were split into separate controls). Rewrite it so it names no Act and no section. Keep "data principals" wording.
- how_to_ask: reuse the curated guidance where good, rewritten in your own words.
- Output files: docs/ucf/drafts/fields_<DOMAIN>.csv for the five privacy domains (PNC, PRT, PUR, PGV, PSC); for the remainder slots in base domains write docs/ucf/drafts/fields_<DOMAIN>_dpdpa.csv (for example fields_GOV_dpdpa.csv). Do not touch existing fields files.
