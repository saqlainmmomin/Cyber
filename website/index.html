Why Yozora

Assessments take a team, vary by reviewer, and leak into personal AI tools.

Each of these is a cost today. Together they are why assessments are slower, riskier and harder to trust than they should be.

Five roles, one assessment.

Evidence chasers, analysts, reviewers and report writers each hold a piece, and someone has to keep them in step. A lot of the calendar time goes on handoffs, not analysis.

Two reviewers, two answers.

Findings and scoring shift with whoever did the work, so results are hard to compare between clients, repeat next year, or defend to an auditor.

Status lives in someone's head.

Evidence is in a shared folder, answers are in a spreadsheet, findings are in email. Asking where an engagement stands means asking a person.

The shortcut nobody approved.

Because it is faster, people paste client documents into personal AI accounts to get the work done. The benefit is real, and so is the exposure.

What changes

An assessment that took a team can be led by one reviewer.

Yozora handles the coordination and the first draft. Your experts spend their time on judgment.

Security and trust

Bring the AI your team already uses into a controlled workspace.

People are already using AI to get through assessments. Yozora gives that work a proper home, with guardrails that make the results defensible.

The goal: keep the speed of AI-assisted work while keeping client documents in one managed place, not scattered across personal tools.

One managed place for client evidence

Documents, answers and findings live in the assessment itself, not in chat histories and personal accounts.

Scores are never generated by a model

The model drafts qualitative findings. Scores come from deterministic rules, so the same evidence always gives the same result.

Every finding traces back to evidence

A reviewer can see exactly what a finding was based on, which is what makes it defensible to a client or an auditor.

People make the final call

Yozora drafts and organises. A qualified reviewer confirms every conclusion before it reaches a report.

Any standard

Built for any framework, not tied to one.

Yozora treats a standard as data. Six are available today, and new standards or custom assessments can be added without changing how the platform works. Controls are mapped into shared clusters, so evidence given once counts wherever it applies.
