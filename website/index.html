Why we exist

Compliance assessments are slow, repetitive and hard to compare. The same control gets re-checked for every framework, findings depend on who reviewed them, and the board gets a spreadsheet. We're building a platform where the evidence leads, the scoring is consistent, and the report is something a board will actually read.

How it works

Evidence first

Uploaded documents are read and used to pre-fill the assessment, so teams only answer what the evidence can't.

Adaptive questionnaire

Scope and tier determine which questions are asked, so small organisations aren't buried in enterprise-grade forms.

AI-assisted gap analysis

Claude drafts qualitative gap findings tied back to the evidence, for a human reviewer to confirm.

Deterministic scoring

Scores are calculated by fixed rules, never by a model, so results are repeatable and explainable.

One control, many frameworks

Controls are de-duplicated across frameworks, so one answer counts wherever it applies.

Board-ready reports

PDF reports and evidence request lists, generated from the reviewed assessment.
