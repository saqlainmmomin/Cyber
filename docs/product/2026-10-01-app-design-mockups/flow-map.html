Engagement flow map

How one engagement moves from creation to a released report, where each screen lives, and what the main button is at every stage. Approved by Saqlain on 2 Oct 2026; the mockups follow it (see screens/IA-SPEC.md).

How the app is organised

Four levels. Every page's breadcrumb shows the levels above it, so you always know which client, engagement and assessment you are in.

The journey

The five stepper stages of an assessment, with the creation steps before it and the loop that brings work back: missing evidence goes back to the client as a request. Approved gaps branch off into findings and actions.

Stage by stage

The main button is the one primary on the assessment Overview at that stage; it moves forward as the work does. Screen names link to the current mockups, some of which change once this is approved.

What changes from the current mockups

  • One stepper, on the assessment only: Scope, Evidence, Questionnaire, Review, Report. The engagement page had a second, different stepper; it goes. The engagement Overview shows each assessment's current stage and next action instead.
  • Evidence is one place. The assessment Documents tab, the engagement AWS evidence tab and the client links page become one Evidence tab on the engagement: an Inventory view (every item with its source, status and the requirements it supports) and a Requests view (what was asked of the client and what has arrived). Upload, AWS pull and reuse are actions on that screen, not separate tabs.
  • Remediation moves out from beside AWS evidence into its own Findings and actions tab.
  • Review gets a tab. Conclusions, the review queue, findings and the workpaper were floating pages with half breadcrumbs; they sit under the assessment Review tab.
  • Reports split by level. Assessment Report tab: one assessment's report, sign-off, versions and applicability. Engagement Reports tab: the integrated report across assessments and comparisons.
  • Breadcrumbs are always complete: Client / Engagement / Assessment / page.
  • Retention leaves client and engagement pages for firm Settings, under data housekeeping. The engagement keeps only an Archive action.
  • Evidence status uses the app's real states: Scanning (quarantined until the malware scan clears it), Available, Rejected, Out of date. The app has no separate in-review or accepted state for evidence; acceptance happens on the conclusions that cite it.

Decisions, approved 2 Oct 2026

  • Structure approved: engagement tabs Overview, Evidence, Findings and actions, Reports; assessment tabs Overview, Scope, Questionnaire, Review, Report; one five-stage stepper on the assessment.
  • Evidence lives on the engagement only. The assessment's Evidence step opens it filtered to that assessment.
  • An engagement with one assessment opens straight on that assessment. The engagement tabs stay one click away in the breadcrumb.
  • Kept features the app does not have yet: Add assessment, Export actions, Open current report, and Email the firm (which needs a new contact email field in firm Settings).
  • Pre-fill sits on the Questionnaire tab (decided 3 Oct 2026): it runs per assessment and its output is the pre-filled answers, so it starts the questionnaire. The Evidence list only notes new documents since the last pre-fill.
