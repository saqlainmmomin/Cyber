Executive summary

Compare with last year

Meridian Ledger Technologies has the foundations of a DPDPA programme in place: a record of processing, a named data protection officer and an incident register. The largest gaps are at the points where data principals act. Consent can be withdrawn only by emailing the officer, and the breach procedure has no route to notify affected principals.

Four requirements are non-compliant and seven are partly met. Two could not be assessed because no evidence was provided. Closing the six critical and high gaps would remove most of the exposure under the Act.
