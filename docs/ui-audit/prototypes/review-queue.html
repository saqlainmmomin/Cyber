A8.13 Information backup · Technological

Backup recovery has not been demonstrated

Approved
Compliance
Partially compliant
Risk
High
Decision
Approved
Proposed by
AI, from cited evidence

Gap

Backup job success alone does not establish that data can be restored within the required recovery point and time.

Impact if unresolved

Without demonstrated recovery, a backup failure could prolong interruption of logistics operations.

Recommendation

Perform the scheduled database restore, record sample integrity and recovery time, and retest quarterly.

Why this recommendation

An evidenced restore test checks recovery capability rather than the existence of backups alone.

Reasoning on record

Nightly backups ran, but there was no restore test in the quarter and failover did not restore a retained copy. Backup job success alone does not establish that data can be restored within the required recovery point and time.
