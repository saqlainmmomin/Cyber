Executive summary

Veldhara’s July to September engagement reviewed privacy and information security. This ISO 27001 view uses the approved control conclusions and their linked evidence at the 6 October cut-off. Physical safeguards are the strongest assessed area. Organizational controls are the weakest, with incomplete access reviews and untested incident response. The ISO score is 84%, but it should be read alongside those high-risk gaps. The separate DPDPA result is 44%; the two scores are not combined.
