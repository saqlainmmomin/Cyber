| UC-PHY-01 | **Physical security perimeters**<br>A physical perimeter is defined for each site in scope, such as the building, floor or server room, stating the protection each one needs. At least once a cycle, the perimeters are confirmed to exist as described, for example that doors lock and there are no unprotected openings. Where the organization has no premises of its own, this is recorded and reliance is placed on the hosting provider's assurance. This keeps unauthorized people away from the areas that hold information and assets. | Head of Facilities<br>high<br>Physical | Select a sample of in-scope sites or areas and compare each perimeter definition with a walkthrough record or photo.
