| UC-PHY-01 | **Physical security perimeters**<br>A physical perimeter is defined for each site in scope, such as the building, floor or server room, stating the protection each one needs. At least once a cycle, the perimeters are confirmed to exist as described, for example that doors lock and there are no unprotected openings. Where the organization has no premises of its own, this is recorded and reliance is placed on the hosting provider's assurance. This keeps unauthorized people away from the areas that hold information and assets. | Head of Facilities<br>high<br>Physical | Select a sample of in-scope sites or areas and compare each perimeter definition with a walkthrough record or photo. Pass if every sampled perimeter is defined, matches what was observed, and no unprotected opening is found. Cover the current review period. | ISO: A.7.1 (full) |  | [ ] |
| UC-PHY-02 | **Physical entry controls**<br>Entry to each secure area is controlled through locks, badge readers or reception checks, with written rules on who may enter. Every visitor is recorded and escorted where the rules require. On a regular cycle, who holds access to secure areas is reviewed and leavers' badges are deactivated. This keeps secure areas limited to people with a current need, and the visitor logs and access reports prove it. | Head of Facilities<br>high<br>Physical | Sample visitor entries and access system records for the period, and a sample of leavers. Pass if entry rules exist, sampled visitors are logged and escorted as required, the latest access review is on file with findings actioned, and every sampled leaver's badge is deactivated. | Entry control rules for secure areas; visitor logs; access control system reports; physical access review records; badge deactivation records for leavers. Cover the review period. | ISO: A.7.2 (full)<br>NIST: PR.AA-06-a,c (full) |  | [ ] |

This reduces the chance that sensitive rooms are found, entered or observed by people with no need. | Head of Facilities<br>medium<br>Physical | Select a sample of rooms holding sensitive information or equipment and compare each with the specification, using inspection records or a walkthrough. This lowers the chance that a natural event or attack on a site destroys information or equipment. | Head of Facilities<br>medium<br>Physical | Select a sample of sites and compare each assessment with the installed protection.
