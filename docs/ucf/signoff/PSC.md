| UC-PSC-01 | **Age verification**<br>Onboarding determines whether a user is a child, meaning a person under 18, before the organization processes the user's data beyond what that check needs. The result of the age declaration or check is stored against the account, and accounts identified as a child's are flagged so the child protections apply. A sample of onboarding records is reviewed on a defined cycle. Proof is the onboarding design and the sampled account records. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Walk through the onboarding flow and sample onboarding records from the audit period. Pass if every sampled record has an age result stored against the account and accounts identified as a child's are flagged. | Onboarding flow design or screenshots; age declaration or check results stored against accounts; account flagging rules; sample of onboarding records for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.9-a (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PSC-02 | **Verifiable guardian consent**<br>Verifiable consent is obtained from a parent before a child's personal data is processed, or from a lawfully appointed guardian for a person with a disability, and the consent is recorded before the data is first processed. The person consenting is checked to be an identifiable adult, using details already reliably held or details from an authorized identity source. For a guardian, the appointment by a court or a designated authority is checked. Proof is the consent and verification records. | Data Protection Officer<br>critical | Sample child accounts from the audit period. Check each has a parental or guardian consent record, a verification method and a consent date before the first processing. Pass if every sampled account does, or has a recorded exemption. | Parental consent process; guardian verification procedure; consent and verification records for sampled child accounts; records of any recorded exemptions the organization relies on, for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.9(1) (full); s.9-b (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PSC-03 | **No tracking or targeting of children**<br>The organization does not track children, monitor their behavior or direct targeted advertising at them, except where a recorded exemption allows it. A written rule prohibits these practices, and analytics and ad-tech settings for child accounts have behavioral tracking and ad personalization switched off. Where an exemption is relied on, its class or purpose and its condition are recorded. Proof is the rule, the configuration records and the exemption record. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Read the rule. Sample child accounts and inspect analytics and ad-tech configuration. Pass if tracking and ad personalization are off for every sampled child account and every exemption relied on is recorded. | Policy prohibiting tracking and targeted advertising of children; analytics and ad-tech configuration screenshots for child accounts; exemption record; age flagging records for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.9(3) (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PSC-04 | **No harmful processing of children's data**<br>The organization's processing of children's personal data is assessed for likely harm to their well-being and the result is recorded. Processing that the assessment finds likely to harm well-being is prohibited or blocked by a documented control, such as a product or content rule. The assessment is repeated when processing of children's data starts or changes. Proof is the assessment record and the blocking control. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Read the assessment and the product or content policy. Sample processing it marks as harmful and check a documented control blocks it. Pass if the assessment is current and every sampled harmful processing is prohibited or blocked. | Children's data assessment; product or content policy; records of features blocked or changed; assessment review records for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.9(2) (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PSC-05 | **Transfers only to permitted destinations**<br>Government notifications that restrict transfers of personal data to specific countries or territories are tracked and kept current. Before personal data is sent outside India, the destination is checked against the current restrictions, and transfers to a restricted destination are stopped. The check is updated when a new notification is published. Proof is the notification tracker and the destination check records. | Legal Counsel<br>high | Obtain the tracker and the list of destinations in the flow inventory. Check each destination against current restrictions and sample hosting regions of systems holding personal data. Pass if no sampled destination is restricted and the tracker is current and was reviewed recently. | Notification tracker; destination check procedure; transfer approvals or check records; hosting region configuration for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.16(1)-a (full); s.16-a,b (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PSC-06 | **Cross-border flow inventory**<br>An inventory lists every flow of personal data outside India, naming the destination country and the recipient for each flow. It is updated whenever a new flow starts or a recipient changes, and reconciled with system hosting regions on a defined cycle. Proof is the inventory and the reconciliation records. | Data Governance Lead<br>medium | Obtain the inventory. Sample systems that hold personal data and compare their hosting regions and external recipients with it. Pass if every sampled flow is listed with destination and recipient and the hosting regions match. | Cross-border flow inventory with destination and recipient; data flow diagrams; cloud hosting configuration; reconciliation records for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.16(1)-b (full) | Privacy-only. May relate to AST-06 (transfer channels) but the test is the destination inventory. | [ ] |
| UC-PSC-07 | **Local storage and sectoral transfer rules**<br>A register lists each law or regulator direction that requires categories of personal data to be stored in India or limits where they may be sent, beyond the general destination rules. For each requirement, the register records the affected data and its storage location, and a control keeps that data, including backups and disaster recovery copies, in India. The register is reviewed on a defined cycle. Proof is the register and the hosting configuration. | Legal Counsel<br>high | Read the register. For each requirement, check the documented storage location and inspect hosting configuration including backups and recovery copies. Pass if every sampled requirement maps to data stored only in India. | Register of storage and transfer requirements; data storage location records; cloud hosting configuration including backup and recovery regions; register review records for the audit period. | DPDPA: s.16-c (full); s.16 (full) | Privacy-only. Applies where such requirements exist. | [ ] |
