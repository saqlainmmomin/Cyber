| UC-RES-03 | **Recovery plan testing**<br>IT recovery plans are tested at least yearly. After each test, the results are compared with the recovery objectives, and any gap is logged with an owner and a due date. This shows whether recovery works in practice and drives fixes before a real outage. The test reports and the gap actions are the proof. | Business Continuity Manager<br>high<br>Organizational | Take the critical services and confirm each recovery plan was tested within the last year. For the latest test reports check that results are compared with the objectives and that every gap has an action. | UC-RES-05 | **Restore testing**<br>Restore tests run at the frequency set in the backup policy, and each result is recorded. The restore test records are the proof. | Business Continuity Manager<br>high<br>Technological | Read the restore test frequency in the backup policy.
