# Suppliers and third parties

Owner role: Head of Procurement. Domain code: SUP.

9 controls. Shown with ISO 27001: 6. Shown with NIST CSF: 7. Shown with DPDPA: 2. Shared by two or more frameworks: 5.

| UC-SUP-40 | **Supplier risk management program**<br>A documented supplier risk program states its objectives and covers supplier security from selection through contracting and monitoring to exit. The program is formally approved, and the responsibilities are communicated to the third parties. This gives every supplier decision one agreed basis. Proof is the program document, the approval record and a shared-responsibility matrix or contract wording. | Head of Procurement<br>high | Obtain the program document and confirm it states objectives and covers selection, contracting, monitoring and exit. | UC-SUP-41 | **Supplier relationship exit**<br>Supplier contracts or the supplier risk plan set out what must happen when a relationship ends, such as return or destruction of data, removal of access and transition support. Proof is the exit clauses and the offboarding records for suppliers that left. | Head of Procurement<br>medium | Obtain the exit clauses or plan and confirm they cover data return or destruction and access removal. Identify suppliers that left in the review period and sample their offboarding records for completed steps. Pass if the exit terms exist and each sampled exit shows the steps done, or the organization shows that none ended. | Contract templates or exit clauses; supplier exit or offboarding procedure; offboarding records for suppliers that left, showing access removal and data return or destruction confirmation; or a statement that no relationship ended; covering the last annual cycle. | NIST: GV.SC-10 (full) | NIST-only.
