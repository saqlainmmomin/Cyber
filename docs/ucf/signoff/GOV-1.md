| UC-GOV-01 | **Internal and external issues**<br>A register of the internal and external issues that affect the organization's security outcomes is kept, including climate change where it is relevant. | UC-GOV-02 | **Interested parties and their requirements**<br>A register is kept of the parties with a stake in security, such as customers, regulators, partners and staff. | UC-GOV-03 | **Scope of the security program**<br>The scope of the security program is defined and documented, covering its boundaries and where it applies. | UC-GOV-04 | **Security management system operated**<br>A security management system is established, run and maintained, with defined processes and the way they interact.
