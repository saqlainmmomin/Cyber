| UC-PGV-01 | **Data protection officer appointed**<br>A data protection officer who is an individual based in India is appointed by a formal appointment letter or governing-body resolution. The officer answers to the governing body, with the reporting line documented, and is named as the contact point for the grievance process. The officer reports to the governing body on each reporting cycle. Proof is the appointment record, the organization chart and the governing-body minutes. | Top Management<br>high | Obtain the appointment record and the organization chart. Check the person is an individual based in India, reports to the governing body and is named in the grievance process. Pass if all three hold and minutes from the last reporting cycle show the officer reporting. | Appointment letter or governing-body resolution; organization chart showing the reporting line; grievance process naming the officer as contact; governing-body minutes recording the officer's reports for the last reporting cycle. | DPDPA: s.10(2)(a) (full) | Privacy-only. Applies to organizations designated as significant. Base list has no equivalent role control (GOV-07 is generic). | [x] |
| UC-PGV-02 | **Independent data auditor appointed**<br>An independent data auditor is appointed under a documented engagement to evaluate the organization's compliance with its data protection duties. The auditor has no role in the processing or in designing the controls under audit, and signs a declaration of independence. The auditor issues a report on each audit cycle and the report reaches the governing body. Proof is the engagement letter, the independence declaration and the audit report. | Top Management<br>high | Obtain the engagement letter, independence declaration and latest report. Check the auditor has no role in the processing or controls under audit. Pass if the engagement is documented, independence is declared and a report was issued in the latest audit cycle. | Auditor engagement letter; independence declaration; latest audit report; evidence the report reached the governing body, for the last audit cycle. | DPDPA: s.10(2)(b) (full) | Privacy-only. Applies to significant organizations. | [x] |
| UC-PGV-03 | **Data protection impact assessment**<br>The risks that the organization's processing creates for the rights of data principals are assessed, covering the processing purposes, the rights involved and how each risk is managed, including a check that any algorithmic software used on personal data does not put those rights at risk. The assessment is repeated once every 12 months and a report is kept. A report of its significant observations is given to the Board. Proof is the assessment method, the reports and the Board submission acknowledgement. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Read the method and the reports for each cycle since designation. Check the report covers purposes, rights and risk treatment and that the Board acknowledgement is on file. Pass if an assessment was completed in each 12-month period and the observations reached the Board. | Assessment method; assessment reports for each cycle; algorithm or model risk assessments; Board submission acknowledgements. | DPDPA: s.10(2)(c)(i) (full) | Privacy-only. Applies to significant organizations. | [x] |
| UC-PGV-04 | **Periodic compliance audit and report**<br>An audit of the organization's compliance with its data protection duties is scheduled once every 12 months in an audit plan. The significant observations of the audit are reported to the Board. Findings are tracked to closure and the results are reported to the governing body. Proof is the audit plan, the audit reports and the Board submission acknowledgement. | Head of Internal Audit<br>high | Read the audit plan and the reports for each period since designation. Check the Board acknowledgement. Pass if the plan sets an audit once every 12 months, an audit was completed in each period and the Board received the significant observations. | Audit plan; audit reports for each cycle; Board submission acknowledgements; findings tracker with closure records. | DPDPA: s.10(2)(c)(ii) (full) | Privacy-only. Applies to significant organizations. Close to RSK-09 but audits privacy compliance, not the security program. | [x] |
