| UC-INC-71 | **Breach notice to affected individuals**<br>Each data principal affected by a personal data breach is notified without delay, through the user account or a communication channel the person registered. This applies to every affected person with no harm threshold. Proof is the notification template and the dispatch records. | Incident Response Lead<br>critical | Sample personal data breaches from the audit period.
