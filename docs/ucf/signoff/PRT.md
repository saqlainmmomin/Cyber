| UC-PRT-01 | **Channel for rights requests**<br>The means by which people can ask for access, correction, completion or erasure of their personal data, and the identifier they must give, are published. The published means are checked after each change to the website, the app or the request process. This gives people a route to use their rights. The published pages and the request intake form are the proof. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Check the website and the app for the published means for each of the four request types and for the identifier required. Start one test request through each channel. Pass if each type is covered, the route is findable within a few steps and each test request reaches the request intake. | Website and app pages showing the request route; rights request intake form; description of the identifier required; covering the review period. | DPDPA: s.11(1)-a (full); s.12(3)-a (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PRT-02 | **Summary of data and processing on request**<br>Each access request is answered with a summary of the personal data processed, the processing activities and the other fiduciaries and processors the data was shared with, including what was shared. A response template is used and a request log is kept. This lets people see how their data is used. The log and the sample responses are the proof. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Sample access requests from the review period. For each, check the response includes the data summary, the activities and the recipient information. Where something was withheld, check a legal reason is recorded. Pass if every sampled response is complete or has a recorded lawful reason. | Access request procedure; response template; access request log; sample completed responses; list of recipients shared with; covering the review period. | DPDPA: s.11(1)-b,c,d (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PRT-03 | **Correction and completion**<br>Inaccurate or misleading personal data is corrected, and incomplete data is completed, when a person asks. The change is made in the system of record and passed on to systems that copy the data. Each request is tracked to closure. This keeps decisions about people based on accurate data. The request log and before and after extracts are the proof. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Sample correction and completion requests from the review period. For each, compare the record before and after and confirm the change was made in the system of record. Pass if every accepted request shows the change in place, and every declined request has a recorded reason. | Rights request procedure; request log of correction and completion requests; before and after record extracts; evidence of updates in systems that copy the data; covering the review period. | DPDPA: s.12(1) (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PRT-04 | **Erasure on request**<br>A person's personal data is erased when they ask, and processors erase it too, unless keeping it is necessary for the purpose or the law requires it. The outcome of each request is recorded, either as proof of erasure or as the ground for keeping. This honors the person's right to have data removed. The request log and erasure records are the proof. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Sample erasure requests from the review period. For each, check the closure shows evidence of erasure, including at processors, or a recorded ground for keeping the data. Pass if every sampled request has one or the other and no data remains where erasure was recorded. | Rights request procedure; erasure request log; erasure records or deletion confirmations; processor instruction and confirmation records; retention ground records; covering the review period. | DPDPA: s.12(3)-b (full) | Privacy-only. Deletion mechanics are reached through AST-08 and AST-70. | [ ] |
| UC-PRT-05 | **Grievance channel and owner**<br>An accessible way for people to raise grievances about how their data is handled is provided, and a named person or role handles them. The route is published and a grievance log records each case. This gives people a place to complain before going to the regulator. The published route, the owner appointment and the grievance log are the proof. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Check the website and app for the published grievance route and test it with a sample submission. Confirm a named role or person is responsible. Sample entries in the grievance log from the review period. Pass if the route works, an owner is named and every sampled entry has a receipt date, subject and outcome. | Published grievance route on the website and app; appointment of the grievance handler; grievance log with receipt date, subject and outcome; covering the review period. | DPDPA: s.13(1) (full) | Privacy-only. | [ ] |
| UC-PRT-06 | **Grievance response and escalation notice**<br>Each grievance is answered within a published period of no more than 90 days. Due dates are tracked and items close to the limit are escalated. The response tells the person they may approach the Board if the grievance stays unresolved. This keeps complaints moving and the escalation right visible. The grievance log and the response template are the proof. | Data Protection Officer<br>high | Sample grievances from the review period and compare receipt and response dates with the published period. Read the responses for the statement about the Board. Pass if the published period is no more than 90 days, every sampled grievance was answered within it and every response mentions the Board. | Published grievance response period; grievance log with receipt and response dates; response template mentioning the Board; ticketing or alert configuration; escalation records; covering the review period. | DPDPA: s.13(2) (full) | Privacy-only. Time limit (dpdpa layer) goes in key_settings if one is fixed. | [ ] |
